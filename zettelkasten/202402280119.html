Higher-Self Motivations

Your higher-self motivations are those that come from a selfless place of love and purpose greater than yourself. Refer to the top of Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs.

Examples

  1. Serving God.
  2. Donating to charity.
  3. Helping your family.
  4. Being kind.
  5. Desire to alleviate other people's suffering.
