Pane Commands in WSL

  1. Creating Panes
  2. Alt + Shift + - creates a new vertical pane.
  3. Alt + Shift + + creates a new horizontal pane.
  4. Closing Panes
  5. Ctrl + Shift + w closes the focused/selected pane. If there is only one pane, WSL is closed.
  6. Resizing Panes
  7. Shift + Alt + Arrow-Key resizes the panes depending on the direction of the arrow-key pressed.
